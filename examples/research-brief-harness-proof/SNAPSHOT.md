# Research Brief

## Scope
- Requested outcome: Produce a compact, traceable research brief on reliable adaptation of embodied agents under distribution shift, with a bounded reading path and explicit open risks.
- Evidence boundary: this is a focused orientation based on 12 selected papers, not an exhaustive literature claim.
- Comparison lenses: Problem Settings & Embodiment, Model & Policy Architectures, Data, Training & Post-Training, Evaluation, Safety & Deployment.

## Key themes
- Studies test-time policy adaptation for embodied agents facing lighting, texture, and camera shifts, with emphasis on stability and bounded updates [P0001 - Robust Policy Adaptation Under Visual Distribution Shift (https://example.org/papers/robust-policy-adaptation)].
- Defines evaluation protocols that report recovery time, regression risk, safety violations, and retained capabilities [P0002 - Failure-Aware Evaluation Protocols for Embodied Adaptation (https://example.org/papers/failure-aware-evaluation)].
- Benchmarks adapters and low-rank updates for vision-language-action models under new tasks and environments [P0003 - Parameter-Efficient Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action Models (https://example.org/papers/peft-vla)].
- Defines evaluation splits for appearance, dynamics, morphology, and task-composition shifts in embodied-agent benchmarks [P0004 - Benchmarking Embodied Agents Across Domain Shifts (https://example.org/papers/embodied-domain-shift-benchmark)].
- Combines episodic memory retrieval with constrained policy updates for long-horizon household tasks [P0005 - Memory-Guided Adaptation in Long-Horizon Household Tasks (https://example.org/papers/memory-guided-adaptation)].
- Uses predictive world models to test candidate adaptations before applying them to a deployed embodied policy [P0006 - World Models for Rapid Embodied Adaptation (https://example.org/papers/world-model-adaptation)].
