# Research Brief

## Scope
- Requested outcome: Produce a compact, traceable research brief on reliable adaptation of embodied agents under distribution shift, with a bounded reading path and explicit open risks.
- Evidence boundary: this is a focused orientation based on 12 selected papers, not an exhaustive literature claim.
- Comparison lenses: Problem Settings & Embodiment, Model & Policy Architectures, Data, Training & Post-Training, Evaluation, Safety & Deployment.

## Key themes
- The study reviews 3D generation for embodied AI and organizes the literature around three roles that 3D generation plays in embodied systems [P0001 - 3D Generation for Embodied AI and Robotic Simulation: A Survey (http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26509v3)].
- Experiments on public humanoid teleoperation dataset show that the proposed method improves the tradeoff between reconstruction error and communication energy consumption under sim-to-real distribution shift [P0002 - Domain-Adaptive Communication-Rate Optimization for Sim-to-Real Humanoid-Robot Wireless XR Teleoperation (http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19293v1)].
- Biologically inspired, the study presents a framework for online Continual Reinforcement Learning that enables automated adaptation during deployment [P0003 - Self-adapting Robotic Agents through Online Continual Reinforcement Learning with World Model Feedback (http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04029v1)].
- The authors propose Contrastive Forward Dynamics loss, which combines dynamics model learning with time-contrastive techniques [P0004 - Self-Supervised Sim-to-Real Adaptation for Visual Robotic Manipulation (http://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09470v1)].
- The authors propose X-Sim, a real-to-sim-to-real framework that uses object motion as a dense and transferable signal for learning robot policies [P0005 - X-Sim: Cross-Embodiment Learning via Real-to-Sim-to-Real (http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07096v5)].
- Addressing this issue, the authors propose AdaptSim, a new task-driven adaptation framework for sim-to-real transfer that aims to optimize task performance in target (real) environments -- instead of matching dynamics between simulation and reality [P0006 - AdaptSim: Task-Driven Simulation Adaptation for Sim-to-Real Transfer (http://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04903v2)].
